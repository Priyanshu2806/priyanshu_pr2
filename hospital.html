Specialized Medical Departments

We offer a wide range of medical departments to cater to all your healthcare needs.

General Physician

Comprehensive primary care with diagnosis and treatment for common health conditions.

Cardiology

Expert heart care including diagnostics, interventional procedures, and cardiac surgery.
